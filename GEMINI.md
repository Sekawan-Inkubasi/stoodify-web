## antislop
Always follow the Anti-Slop principles whenever generating or editing code, UI/UX, or copywriting.
The goal: the design and copy must feel **crafted by an intentional human designer**, never generated by default AI aesthetics.

- **Core Anti-Slop Filter**: `~/.gemini/config/skills/antislop/SKILL.md`
- **UI & Visual Design**: `~/.gemini/config/skills/antislop-ui/SKILL.md` (no generic purple-blue gradients, no floaty cards, no arbitrary borders, intentional typography & whitespace)
- **Copywriting**: `~/.gemini/config/skills/antislop-copywriting/SKILL.md` (no AI fluff, no buzzwords, concise and human voice)
- **Accessibility & Contrast**: `~/.gemini/config/skills/antislop-human/SKILL.md` (WCAG compliance, keyboard focus, contrast checking via `antislop-contrast` MCP)
- **Mobile & Responsive Layout**: `~/.gemini/config/skills/antislop-layoutmobile/SKILL.md` (fluid responsive behavior, tap targets, no horizontal overflow)
- **Code Hygiene**: `~/.gemini/config/skills/antislop-code/SKILL.md` (no redundant or obvious AI comments)
